02 / how it started

@stitchrzz floated the idea of rerunning dogecoin paired with a stablecoin. @billym2k replied that y’all can do whatever you want, and welcomed creator-reward support. so it launched on solana, and the project states that 100% of creator rewards go to shibetoshi nakamoto.

03 / arcade

in 1984 a twelve-year-old elon musk wrote a shooter called blastar. this is a small, original homage in the doge universe: shoot the freighters, dodge the beams, grab $ for a shield. no wallet, no rewards, just a high score.
